Research

I am interested in tactile sensing, robotic manipulation, and robot learning. My current research explores how visuo-tactile information can support object reconstruction for contact-rich manipulation.

Learning Visuo-Tactile Perception for Contact-rich Robotic Manipulation

M.Sc. dissertation · National University of Singapore · 2026-2027

Developing a visuo-tactile UMI and a contact-rich manipulation dataset to study whether multimodal representations improve robustness over vision-only approaches.

Long-horizon Cloth Folding with SFT and DAgger

PrimeBOT Research Institute · 2026

Studied policy generalization for long-horizon cloth-folding tasks using supervised fine-tuning and dataset aggregation, achieving an 85% task success rate.

Edge AI-based Heart Sound Diagnosis System

Final-year project · 2025-2026
