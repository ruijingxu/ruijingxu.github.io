# Ruijing Xu Academic Homepage Design

## Goal

Publish a concise academic homepage for Ruijing Xu at `ruijingxu.github.io`, reusing the classic structure and interaction style of Jon Barron's public academic website.

## Scope

- Keep the site as dependency-free static HTML and CSS hosted by GitHub Pages.
- Preserve the reference site's centered, narrow single-page layout, circular portrait, blue links, and research-list typography.
- Replace all personal content and media from the reference site with Ruijing Xu's information.
- Include an About introduction, research interests, selected experience and research items derived from the supplied CV.
- Add working Email, CV, GitHub, and LinkedIn links.
- Add the supplied portrait as the profile image.
- State that Ruijing is open to research assistant and internship opportunities in Singapore and China.
- Leave room for future project, lab, and publication links without publishing empty or fake links.

## Content

The About section will use the user-approved wording: Ruijing is an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at NUS, supervised by Prof. Xingyu Liu, with interests in robotics and embodied AI. It will mention the B.Eng. from NJUST, the exchange at UPC in Barcelona, and internships at PrimeBOT Research Institute and Senad Robotics AI Research Institute.

The Research section will describe interests in tactile sensing, robotic manipulation, robot learning, and visuo-tactile object reconstruction for contact-rich manipulation. Initial entries will cover the current NUS visuo-tactile research, the PrimeBOT long-horizon cloth-folding project, and the edge-AI heart-sound project.
